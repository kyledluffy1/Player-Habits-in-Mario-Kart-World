# Player Habits in Mario Kart World

## Overview

This master's capstone project investigated behavioral differences between casual and competitive players in Mario Kart World.

The study used a custom-designed survey containing 32 questions and collected over 600 responses from players.

## Research Questions

- What motivates players to play Mario Kart World?
- How do competitive and casual players differ?
- Which game modes are preferred by different player groups?
- What factors contribute to long-term engagement?

## Methodology

- Designed a 32-question survey
- Collected 600+ responses
- Cleaned and standardized survey data
- Created visualizations and statistical summaries
- Produced a 9-page research paper

## Skills Demonstrated

- Survey Design
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Research Methods
- Technical Writing
- Excel
- Google Forms

## Results

Key findings included:

- Competitive players reported significantly higher play frequency.
- Casual players favored social play experiences.
- Different motivations were associated with different engagement patterns.
